We build gecko-adhesive tethers that carefully de-tumble space junk.

How it works

  1. Approach — The tether is thrown or extended toward the target, no alignment required.
  2. Contact — A gecko‑inspired pad meets the surface; setae conform to microscale texture.
  3. Adhere — Shear loading engages van der Waals contact — strong grip, zero suction or glue.
  4. Release — A peel angle change drops adhesion to near zero on command.
