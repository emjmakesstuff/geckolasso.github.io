How it works

Approach

The tether is thrown or extended toward the target, no alignment required.

Contact

A gecko‑inspired pad meets the surface; setae conform to microscale texture.

Adhere

Shear loading engages van der Waals contact — strong grip, zero suction or glue.

Release

A peel angle change drops adhesion to near zero on command.
